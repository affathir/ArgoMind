SECTION: POTATO (Solanum tuberosum) — COMPLETE CULTIVATION GUIDE
==============================================================================

## OVERVIEW
Potato is primarily grown in highland areas of Indonesia (Dieng, Karo, Garut) at
elevations of 1000–3000 meters above sea level. It is the most disease-susceptible
crop in the Solanaceae family. Late blight (Phytophthora infestans) is responsible
for up to 80% of crop losses in Indonesia if not managed correctly.

---

## OPTIMAL GROWING CONDITIONS

Soil moisture: 60–80% (uniform moisture critical — tuber cracking and hollow heart if fluctuating)
Soil pH: 5.0–6.5 (optimal 5.5–6.0; potato is more acid-tolerant than most crops)
Air temperature: 15–22°C (optimal 18–20°C; above 28°C tuberization stops)
Air humidity: 70–85%
Sunlight: minimum 8 hours per day (photoperiod affects tuberization)
Altitude: 1000–3000 m above sea level (ideal for Indonesia)
Growing period: 90–120 days

---

## GROWTH STAGES & WATER MANAGEMENT

Planting / Sprouting (0–20 days):
- Soil moisture: 60–70%
- Plant certified seed tubers with 2–3 eyes per piece
- Temperature must be 15–22°C for proper sprouting
- Do not waterlog at planting — seed rot risk

Vegetative growth (20–50 days):
- Soil moisture: 65–75%
- Hill up soil around base of stems at 30 cm height (2–3 times)
- Earthing up prevents green tubers (solanine formation)

Tuberization — MOST CRITICAL STAGE (50–75 days):
- Soil moisture: 70–80% (critical — do not let drop below 55%)
- Low temperature + short days promote tuber initiation
- Drought at this stage causes irregularly shaped tubers and hollow heart
- If soil_moisture < 50% during tuberization: irrigate immediately

Tuber bulking (75–100 days):
- Soil moisture: 65–75%
- Consistent moisture prevents tuber cracking and secondary growth
- Monitor closely for late blight — highest risk period

Maturation (100–120 days):
- Reduce irrigation — soil moisture: 50–60%
- 2 weeks before harvest: stop irrigation completely (skin set)
- Haulm (foliage) destruction 10 days before harvest helps skin hardening

## DISEASE MANAGEMENT FOR POTATO

Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans) — MOST CRITICAL:
- Trigger: temperature 15–22°C + humidity > 90% + rainfall > 5 mm
- Symptoms: water-soaked dark lesions on leaves, white mold underside, rapid spread
- Can destroy entire crop in 7–10 days if untreated
- Treatment: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (systemic) every 5–7 days during rainy season
- PREVENTIVE spraying is essential — do not wait for symptoms

Early Blight (Alternaria solani):
- Trigger: high temperature (25–30°C), aging lower leaves, nutritional stress
- Symptoms: dark concentric ring lesions with yellow halo
- Treatment: Chlorothalonil or Iprodione fungicide

Common Scab (Streptomyces scabies):
- Trigger: soil pH > 6.5, dry conditions during tuberization
- Symptoms: rough corky lesions on tuber surface
- Prevention: keep soil pH between 5.0–6.0 (most effective control)
- Increase soil moisture during tuberization

Bacterial Soft Rot (Erwinia carotovora):
- Trigger: waterlogged soil + warm temperature
- Symptoms: soft, water-soaked, foul-smelling tuber rot
- Prevention: strict drainage management, avoid mechanical damage at harvest

Black Leg (Erwinia carotovora subsp. atroseptica):
- Trigger: wet soil + infected seed tubers
- Symptoms: black discoloration of stem base, plant yellowing
- Use certified disease-free seed tubers only

## FERTILIZATION SCHEDULE

Basal (at planting):
- Organic compost: 20–30 tons/hectare (potato is a heavy feeder)
- SP36: 250 kg/hectare
- KCl: 200 kg/hectare (potassium is critical for tuber quality)
- Dolomite: 1 ton/hectare if pH below 5.0

First top-dressing (25–30 days):
- Urea: 150 kg/hectare
- NPK 15-15-15: 150 kg/hectare

Second top-dressing (50–55 days — before tuberization):
- KCl: 150 kg/hectare (increases tuber starch and reduces hollow heart)
- Calcium nitrate: 100 kg/hectare

Foliar spray during tuber bulking:
- Potassium phosphite: improves disease resistance + late blight suppression
- Boron + Molybdenum: improves internal tuber quality

---

## IRRIGATION DECISION GUIDE FOR POTATO

Sensor reading → Action:
- soil_moisture < 50%: Irrigate immediately — tuber deformity risk
- soil_moisture 50–60%: Irrigate within 12 hours
- soil_moisture 60–80%: Normal condition
- soil_moisture > 85%: Stop irrigation — late blight + bacterial soft rot risk

Late blight weather alert:
- If temperature 15–22°C AND humidity > 85% AND rainfall > 5 mm → spray preventive fungicide immediately

---

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
1. Planting after tomato, chili, or eggplant (same disease reservoir)
2. Using non-certified seed tubers (main source of virus and late blight)
3. Not earthing up (green tubers = toxic solanine)
4. Skipping fungicide during overcast humid weather (late blight spreads fastest)
5. Harvesting in wet soil conditions (skin damage + storage rot)
6. Not drying tubers before storage (storage rot risk)
