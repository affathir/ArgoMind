## 1.1 Blast Disease (Pyricularia oryzae)
Affected crops: Rice

Symptoms:
- Diamond-shaped or rhombus spots on leaves, grayish-brown in color
- Yellow or orange borders around lesions
- Severe infections cause leaves to dry from tip to base
- Neck blast: panicle stem breaks at node, causing empty grains

Environmental conditions favoring blast:
- Air humidity below 40% (dry conditions)
- Air temperature between 28–35°C
- Soil moisture below 25%
- Very low rainfall (less than 2 mm/day)
- Heavy morning dew

Prevention:
- Maintain soil moisture above 40% through regular irrigation
- Use blast-resistant rice varieties (Ciherang, Inpari 32)
- Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilization
- Plant with adequate spacing to improve air circulation

Treatment:
- Spray fungicide with active ingredient Tricyclazole or Isoprothiolane
- For mild infection: dissolve 2 tablespoons baking soda in 1 liter water, spray on leaves
- Remove and burn severely infected plants
- Apply spraying in early morning when dew is still present

## 1.2 Leaf Blight / Brown Spot (Helminthosporium oryzae)
Affected crops: Rice, Corn, Wheat

Symptoms:
- Oval or elongated dark brown spots on leaves
- Pale yellow borders around spots
- In severe cases, spots merge and entire leaves dry out

Favorable conditions:
- High air temperature (30–35°C)
- Soil pH above 7.0 (alkaline tendency)
- Low air humidity (30–40%)
- Overly dense plant spacing

Prevention:
- Maintain soil pH between 5.5–7.0 with proper liming
- Reduce plant density; maintain adequate row spacing
- Avoid excessive irrigation that creates stagnant humid conditions
- Crop rotation to break pathogen cycle

Treatment:
- Spray fungicide with active ingredient Mancozeb or Propiconazole
- Remove infected leaves and destroy them (do not compost)
- Improve field drainage if waterlogging is present

## 1.3 Root Rot (Pythium spp. / Fusarium solani)
Affected crops: All crop types

Symptoms:
- Roots turn dark brown to black, soft, and emit foul odor
- Plant wilts despite wet soil
- Yellowing of leaves from bottom upward
- Stunted growth

Favorable conditions:
- Waterlogged soil (moisture > 80%)
- Very acidic soil pH (below 5.0)
- Low temperature (18–24°C)
- Poor field drainage
- Very high air humidity (> 85%)

Prevention:
- Improve drainage system before planting
- Never allow standing water for more than 24 hours
- Keep soil pH above 5.5 using agricultural lime (dolomite)
- Add organic matter to improve soil structure

Treatment (Act immediately):
- Drastically reduce irrigation
- Open and clear drainage channels immediately
- Apply fungicide with Metalaxyl to root zone
- Add agricultural lime to raise pH if below 5.5
- For severely infected plants: remove and destroy, sterilize soil with 2% formalin

## 1.4 Fusarium Wilt (Fusarium oxysporum)
Affected crops: Tomato, Chili, Watermelon, Melon, Banana

Symptoms:
- Sudden wilting despite moist soil
- Yellowing starting from one side of the plant
- Brown discoloration inside lower stem when cut
- Slow plant death

Favorable conditions:
- Alkaline soil pH (above 7.5)
- High temperature (32–37°C)
- Low soil moisture (15–25%)
- Prolonged drought stress

Prevention:
- Keep soil pH between 5.5–6.5 for susceptible crops
- Use certified disease-free seeds
- Rotate crops every season (avoid continuous Solanaceae planting)
- Add Trichoderma harzianum to soil as biological control agent

Treatment:
- Immediately remove infected plants; do not leave them in the field
- Apply systemic fungicide with Carbendazim to planting hole
- Sterilize soil through solarization (cover with black plastic for 2–4 weeks)
- Sterilize all tools that contacted infected plants

## 1.5 Healthy Crop Indicators

Optimal soil moisture by crop type:
- Rice: 40–70%
- Vegetables: 50–70%
- Fruit crops: 45–65%
- Tree crops: 35–55%

Optimal soil pH by crop type:
- Rice: 5.5–7.0
- Vegetables: 6.0–7.0
- Legumes: 6.0–7.0
- Root crops (cassava, sweet potato): 5.5–6.5

Optimal air temperature:
- Rice: 22–32°C
- Corn: 21–30°C
- Leafy vegetables: 15–25°C
- Chili/Tomato: 22–30°C

Alert thresholds (trigger immediate action):
- Soil moisture < 20%: Critical — irrigate immediately
- Soil moisture > 80%: Critical — improve drainage
- Soil pH < 5.5: Add agricultural lime
- Soil pH > 7.5: Add agricultural sulfur or organic matter
- Air temperature > 38°C: High stress risk — shade and irrigate
- Air humidity < 30%: Increased disease risk
